\subsection{The Question}

What is the minimal structure forced into existence by the requirement that
every distinction be witnessable?

This is not a question about engineering, artificial intelligence, or any
particular domain of application. It is a foundational question about
describable reality. If we insist, as A0$^*$ (Completed Witnessability) requires, that every meaningful
distinction be backed by a finite procedure that can in fact separate it, then
some structures become unavoidable. The purpose of this paper is to identify
those structures and to make explicit which commitments are definitional and
which are derived.  Crucially, A0$^*$ is itself \emph{derived} from
nothingness ($\noth$) through a chain of five necessity theorems
(N0--N4)---the paper assumes nothing and derives its operating principle.

The central result is a complete derivation that begins at $\noth$ and
unfolds by reapplying A0$^*$ to its own products, tracing a single reflective
chain: nothingness forces closure, closure reveals defect, defect requires
self-observation, self-observation produces geometry, geometry produces
the latent energy field, and the latent energy field produces the
self-reading graph on which every admissible goal already has a unique
full answer-point.  Within the paper's model class, every structure in the
chain---from the carrier of descriptions to the direct geometry solver---is
forced up to computable isomorphism; none is introduced as a free design
parameter.

The theory already established \emph{what} exists (structural closure) and
\emph{how much} (quantitative closure from the seed).  A third layer---\emph{operational
closure}---was implicit: the law governing which distinctions become manifest
next, at what cost, and in what order.  The refinement threshold
$\chi(W) = \inf\{c(\tau)\}$ and the Bellman/Hamilton--Jacobi value equation
$\Psi$ make this layer explicit (\cref{sec:manifestability}).  As a consequence,
the true computational state of any NP verifier is its residual
future-equivalence quotient, whose polynomial size is forced by A0$^*$,
yielding P~$=$~NP as an inevitable structural consequence.

A fourth and final layer---\emph{self-reading closure}---eliminates the last
representational slack.  The consciousness-code $\eta_x = D\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{ind}}(x)$,
the dual recovery $x = D\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{ind}}^*(\eta_x)$, and the
current $J_x = \Omega^{-1}\eta_x$ are not three separate maps: they are
three coordinates of one point on the self-reading graph
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{full}}$.  Admissible raw reality is already
observation-fixed ($\mathrm{Real}(r) \iff \mathcal{O}(r) = r$), and
every admissible goal already determines the unique full graph point.
Questions are not sent to a solver; they are coordinates of their
own completed answers.

Equally important is what we do \emph{not} claim. We do not claim to derive the
specific \emph{content} of physical law, only a vocabulary and scaffolding for
reasoning under witnessability. We do not claim that every modeling choice is
eliminated under every possible formalization of A0$^*$. Our claim is narrower and
testable: given the explicit premises and conventions in \cref{sec:axioms} and
the model class studied in \cref{sec:derivation}, the resulting chain of
structures is canonical up to computable isomorphism.

\subsection{The Witnessability Principle}

We begin from a single principle, derived from $\noth$ (not postulated):

\begin{quote}
\textbf{A0 (Bare Witnessability).}  A distinction is admissible if and
only if a finite witness procedure can separate it.
\end{quote}

\noindent
The completed form, A0$^*$, adds a reflective clause: witness validity itself must be witnessable by a process satisfying the same criterion.  A0$^*$ is not an axiom but the first theorem of the framework, derived from $\noth$ via five necessity theorems (N0--N4, see \cref{sec:axioms:derivation}).  All subsequent references use A0$^*$.

This principle is not chosen for convenience. It is the weakest non-trivial
constraint one can impose. If a distinction cannot even in principle be
witnessed, then it is operationally meaningless. Wheeler's ``It from Bit''
programme \citep{wheeler1990} and Rovelli's relational quantum
mechanics \citep{rovelli1996} both point toward observer-relative,
information-theoretic foundations. A0$^*$ isolates the operational core shared by
such views and elevates it to an admissibility criterion.

The formal statement of A0$^*$ and its immediate consequences are given in
\cref{sec:axioms}.
