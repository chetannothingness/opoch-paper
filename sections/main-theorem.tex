\subsection{The Two Strongest Consequences}

\label{thm:goal-coordinate-uniqueness}

$\pi_G(\Sigma(g)) = g$.  Answer, code, current, continuation, and halt

\begin{theorem}[Direct Solvedness]
\label{thm:direct-solvedness}

self-reading graph, not by search, enumeration, or optimization.
The solver $\mathrm{Solve}$ is total, exact, deterministic, and
its output is the unique projection of the graph point selected by
the goal.
