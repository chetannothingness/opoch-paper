\section{Reader's Operational Dictionary}
\label{app:dictionary}

The following terms have exact meanings in the framework.  All are derived
from A0$^*$; none is imported.

\begin{description}[nosep, leftmargin=2em]

\item[Nothingness ($\noth$).]
  The state with no committed distinctions.  Equivalently: maximal
  indistinguishability $\mathfrak{I}_{\max}$.  Not empty structure;
  maximal latent distinction-energy.

\item[Indistinguishability.]
  Two descriptions are indistinguishable if no admissible witness
  separates them.  The equivalence relation forced by A0$^*$.

\item[Latent energy ($\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{ind}}$).]
  The total unrealized distinction-energy of an unresolved class.
  The Bellman recursion on the refinement tree.

\item[Witness.]
  A finite, replayable, self-accounting, endogenously valid procedure
  that separates a distinction.  The fundamental epistemic act.

\item[Closure ($\Pi$).]
  The operation that completes a partial truth quotient under all
  available witnesses.  Extensive, monotone, idempotent.

\item[Truth.]
  The information that survives all valid tests.  The truth quotient
  $\Pi^*(L)$ is the coarsest structure that no test can refine further.

\item[Time.]
  The irreversible formation of records.  Not a parameter; the
  monotone arrow of witness accumulation.

\item[Gauge.]
  Redundancy in description.  Two presentations of the same truth class.
  Gauge-invariant = quotient-invariant.

\item[Seed ($\delta^*$).]
  The unique least positive self-retaining non-gauge closure-defect.
  The first accessible distinction from $\noth$.

\item[Big bang.]
  The first accessible refinement of maximal indistinguishability.
  Not an explosion inside pre-existing space; the first split of $\noth$.

\item[Consciousness.]
  The minimal persistent self-valued witness selector (C1--C4).
  At the deeper level: the locally instantiated, globally determining
  germ of $\mathcal{U}_{\mathrm{ind}}$.

\item[Self-reading graph ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{full}}$).]
  $\{(x, \eta, J, \Gamma, h)\}$: the set of all full points, each
  carrying state, code, current, continuation, and halt as five
  coordinates.

\item[Goal ($g$).]
  An admissible selector form that picks out a unique point of
  $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{full}}$.  A goal IS a coordinate, not an
  instruction to search.

\item[Answer.]
  The state projection $\pi_X(\Sigma(g))$ of the unique graph point
  selected by the goal.  Not computed; read.

\item[Literally solved.]
  For every admissible goal $g$, there exists a unique full graph point
  $\Sigma(g)$, and all answer coordinates are projections.  No search.
  No enumeration.  No optimization.  Coordinate readout.

\end{description}
